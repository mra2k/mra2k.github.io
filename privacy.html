Privacy Policy – MrA2K Clipper

MrA2K Clipper (“the app”) is a personal desktop tool run by the streamer MrA2K on his own computer. It turns his own Twitch stream recordings into short clips and uploads them to his own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no server of its own.

What data the app uses

  • TikTok account data: after you log in with TikTok, the app receives an access token, your open ID, display name and the posting settings TikTok provides (for example allowed privacy levels and the maximum video length).
  • Videos and captions: the clips and texts the app uploads are made from the operator’s own stream recordings.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator’s own videos to his own TikTok account, to show which account a video is posted to, and to check the upload status. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used for profiling.

Where the data is stored

All data, including the TikTok access and refresh tokens, is stored only locally on the operator’s own computer. The app sends data only to TikTok (and, for YouTube uploads, to Google) through their official APIs.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect TikTok in the app. Disconnecting revokes the token at TikTok and deletes it from the computer. You can also remove the app’s access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services permissions.
